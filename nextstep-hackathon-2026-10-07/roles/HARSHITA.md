# Harshita — presentation, story, and audience owner

## Your responsibility

You are not assigned app implementation, terminal setup, or cloud configuration. Your job is to make the real problem and the working result understandable, capture evidence, and protect rehearsal time. You can work independently while the developers mentor students.

## H1 — validate the story, 15–20 focused minutes

Ask 3–5 willing students, without collecting names:

1. “What preparation goal did you last start and then stop?”
2. “What were you supposed to do in your next session?”
3. “After missing a day, did you resume, change the plan, or leave it?”

Record exact anonymous observations in your handoff. This is informal discovery, not a representative survey. If nobody is available, use Darshit's anecdote alone and disclose that limitation. Send the strongest two observations to Darshit without requesting new features.

## H2 — prepare the presentation while implementation runs

Use the script in presentation/PITCH-AND-DEMO.md. Prepare five simple slides in the presentation tool you know best. Prefer large text, one claim per slide, and real screenshots later. Leave clear placeholders until evidence exists.

Prepare: problem hook, user journey, technical architecture, competitor/context answer, current limits, and future pilot. Do not promise GSoC selection, placement success, ADHD treatment, or measured retention improvements.

## H3 — verify comprehension, 10–15 minutes

After G2, ask a student to find today's mission without coaching. Note where they hesitate. Give Darshit at most two high-impact wording/navigation changes; avoid a redesign during the event.

After G3, ask the developers to show preview, Cancel, Apply, and refresh. If a number does not change correctly, report the steps rather than suggesting a technical fix.

## H4 — assets and backup

Capture three actual screenshots: Today, Journey, recovery comparison. Hide credentials and provider key pages. Record a 60–90 second backup of the deployed path after G4. Name assets with date and status, for example `2026-10-07-deployed-recovery.mp4`. Save through your usual tool in presentation/assets/ when ready; no fabricated assets are supplied by this kit.

## H5 — rehearse and submit

- Main narrator: you. Screen operator/product story: Darshit. Technical Q&A: Sankirth.
- First rehearsal: clarity and timing. Second: a failure such as slow backend startup and switching to the labelled recording.
- Keep demo to about two minutes and total pitch to the organiser's actual limit.
- Check submitted URL/QR in a clean browser. Record organiser deadline and submission receipt.
- Confirm the evidence labels with Sankirth; remove unsupported feature claims.
